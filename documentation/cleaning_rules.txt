CLEANING RULES — silver_nanoparticle_experiments.csv

1. Missing tokens (blank, NA, N/A, null, -)
   Rule: convert to NaN, keep the row.
   Note: "none" in stabilizer is a real value, not missing.

2. Temperature units
   Rule: convert everything to °C (K minus 273.15, rounded to the nearest whole number).

3. Temperature outside 0–100 °C
   Rule: set to NaN, add a flag, keep the row.

4. agno3_conc units
   Rule: convert to mM (uM divided by 1000).
   Assumption: plain numbers are already mM.

5. particle_size_nm
   Rule: strip the "nm" suffix, convert to a number.

6. Impossible values
   Rule: set to NaN, add a flag, keep the row.
   Limits: pH 0–14, PDI 0–1, yield 0–100, reaction time > 0, particle size > 0.

7. solvent names
   Rule: strip whitespace, lowercase, map to one of three names:
   water / ethanol / water-ethanol 1:1.

8. date
   Rule: convert to ISO (YYYY-MM-DD).
   Slash dates are day-first (confirmed: first part goes up to 31).

9. Exact duplicate rows
   Rule: drop the extras and log them.

10. Same experiment_id, different particle_size_nm
    Rule: keep the first row, move the other to rejects.csv
    with the reason "conflict, needs review".

11. Provenance
    Rule: add source_row (original CSV row number)
    and cleaning_flags (what was changed or nulled).
